Homeboard

Homeboard is a personal home dashboard. It runs on a Raspberry Pi connected to a monitor in the owner's home and shows a calendar, a task list and the weather on top of rotating background photos.

What it does with your Google account

When the owner links their own Google account, Homeboard asks for read-only access to Google Calendar and Google Tasks. It uses that access only to show the owner's upcoming events and to-do items on the owner's own screen. It cannot change or delete anything in the account.
